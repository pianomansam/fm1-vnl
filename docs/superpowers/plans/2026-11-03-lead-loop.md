> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Jazz-Hop and Ambient leads can play as a sampled record loop (recorded once, replayed identically, with producer moves), switched per song, with a workbench row.

**Architecture:** Port the prototype's shared loop engine (`app/song/leads/loop.c`) into workbench, switched by a new per-song override `lead_loop` instead of the prototype's global/env switch. `leads_plan_bar` routes a looping style's bars through `leads_loop_bar`, which records the style's planned bars once per slot and replays them with harmony checks and moves. The loop's low-pass reaches the lead bus through `song_lead_lp`.

**Tech Stack:** C (firmware, host tests), wasm workbench (wb.c, rows.js, desk.js), Node smoke tests.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-lead-loop-design.md

## Global Constraints

- With the switch off, every song is byte-identical to today: the pinned rolled-song hashes in tests/host/song_test.c do not change.
- Only Jazz-Hop (`LS_JAZZHOP`) and Ambient (`LS_AMBIENT`) loop; every other style ignores the switch.
- No new notes on replay: each replayed note is a recorded note, moved at most 2 scale steps for harmony, or dropped.
- Loop length L = the chord loop's length, 2 or 4 bars (4 at most).
- Live key, mode, chords or set change: re-record from the landing bar (no transpose); its first replay is a new entry.
- Rates (keep the prototype's): first entry filtered in 40%, later entries held back 20% / filtered in 35%, a block straight after another filtered in 15%; muted hook bar 12% of 8-bar passes (never the first); drop before a section change 50% (half whole bar, half last 2 beats); stutter on the turnaround hook bar 40%, a block's last bar 30%, never in the first lead pass; 1/2/3 notes at 55/30/15%; repeats 2-4 (fragments 2-3) in 16ths or 8ths, each 12% softer.
- New: no stutter in the bar right after a stutter.
- Stats and the per-bar move log exist only in host and workbench builds, not in the firmware image.
- Firmware default: a constant `LEAD_LOOP_DEFAULT` = 0 until Sam decides by ear (Task 5).

## Review Focus

- A live set/key change mid-loop: the old phrase must not replay over the new key (test in Task 2: `test_lead_loop_rerecords`).
- Jumping or resuming in the workbench mid-song: the recorded phrase must match playing from the top (wb lands by ticking from bar 0; test in Task 3's smoke check).
- A style change mid-song (workbench lead-style row, or a lead-instrument change re-styling the song): recording state must reset with the style (`style_for` already zeroes `s->lead.st`; zero `s->lead.loop` there too; test in Task 2: `test_lead_loop_rerecords`, second case).
- A song whose lead never plays (no lead blocks): the loop does nothing and costs nothing (Task 2's `test_lead_loop_off_identical` covers off; the on-path is covered by the replay test's seed sweep including such songs).
- Old workbench snapshots without the row restore as Default (Task 3's smoke check).
