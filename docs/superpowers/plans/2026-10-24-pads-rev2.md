# Pads Revision 2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The pad plays either its chord or a solo note per section; the solo note is optional per song and has a
rolled voice (choir, flute, organ, or strings an octave up); the keys gain a half-note comp style.

**Architecture:** The engine lets the pad's lane 4 host its own patch (any sustained model), assigned per song; the
song engine (pads.c, song.c) rolls the song's solo voice and presence and makes sections either/or; harmony.c gains
`CS_HALF`.

**Tech Stack:** C99, float only, no libm, no malloc; host tests `PATH=$PWD/.venv/bin:$PATH sh tests/run_tests.sh`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-pads-design.md`, "Revision 2" (binding) and the first design for
everything it doesn't change.

## Global Constraints

- The engine's rules (ISR-safe, deterministic per seed, denormal-safe); the song engine a pure function of roll and
  plan; new seed streams 191-199 (190 is the section roll).
- Music rules: no semitone rubs with keys or bass; ranges: chord 55-72; solo 72-84 (choir, flute, organ) or 84-96
  (strings); the solo note ignores the lead (Sam).
- Levels: the chord part 7-11 dB under the keys (test_pad_level); lead over keys per-set mean 4-6.5 dB, median 4-6,
  every song ≥ 2 dB; never widen.
- Budget: ≤ about 0.97 on the standard list and seed 20 forced (Sam's accepted line), hard limit 1.0.
- Commits: trailers `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>` and
  `Claude-Session: https://claude.ai/code/session_01ENYN74abncM7Wq3c4vd7md`; never push.
- Deliver render links (with and without the bed) when done; track cost in docs/cost.md.

## Review Focus

1. A section boundary where the chord part hands over to the solo (or back): no stuck note, no gap longer than the
   swell; the outgoing lanes release. Task 2 test.
2. A solo voice whose model differs from M_ENS on lane 4: steals, glides (no glide across a patch change), release at
   the song's end, the carryover test with a choir-solo song in its pairs. Task 1 test.
3. A song whose lead is the flute or whose keys are the organ: the exclusions hold. Task 2 test.
4. Half-note comping over bars_per_chord 2 and a request landing mid-chord: strikes at 0 and 8 only, voicing
   continuous (no revoice on the second strike). Task 3 test.
5. A busy lead over half-note comping: thins to the chord changes. Task 3 test.

### Task 1: The pad's lane 4 hosts its own patch (engine)

**Files:** `app/engine/voices.c`, `app/engine/voices.h`, `app/engine/patch.c` (level trims if needed); Test:
`tests/host/song_test.c`

**Interfaces:** Produces `void vx_assign_pad_solo(int ins);` — lane 4's patch (INS_VOX_PAD, INS_FLUTE, INS_ORGAN or
INS_STRINGS; anything else falls back to the pad's patch); the pad role's pool accepts those models on lane 4 only;
a lane-4 note never glides from a different patch (it starts fresh). A per-patch solo trim (a float table or the
patch level) brings each voice to the strings' loudness on lane 4.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests** `test_pad_solo_patch`: for each of the four voices, a lane-4 note renders with that
  patch's model (no NaN, silent 3 s after release, vx_tiny clean); the four lane-4 RMS levels at MIDI 76 (strings at
  88) within 2 dB of each other; a lane-1 note still M_ENS; the role-bus matrix still passes.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run; fail.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.** Keep the pad bus's filters, sends and quarter-rate path working for a non-M_ENS lane 4
  (a non-M_ENS voice renders at its own model's usual rate into the pad bus).
- [ ] **Step 4: All tests pass; `sh tools/cost-bench.sh --parts` row for each solo voice in docs/cost.md.**
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** `feat(engine): the pad's solo lane takes its own voice`

### Task 2: Either/or sections, an optional solo, a rolled solo voice (song)

**Files:** `app/song/pads.c`, `app/song/pads.h`, `app/song/song.c`; Test: `tests/host/song_test.c`

**Interfaces:** Consumes `vx_assign_pad_solo` (Task 1). Produces `int pads_solo_voice(const song_roll_t *r);` (the
INS_* for the song's solo, or INS_NONE for none; stream 191), `int pads_section_part(const song_roll_t *r, int block,
int section, int sustained_keys);` (0 none, 1 chord, 2 solo), replacing `pads_section_in` where it's used. song.c
assigns the solo voice at song start (vx_assign_pad_solo) and emits per the section's part; strings solos sit in
84-96, the others 72-84.

  songs where allowed; never the flute with a flute lead, never the organ over organ keys; ranges by voice; a handover
  between sections leaves no stuck note (Review Focus 1); determinism; the carryover test gains a choir-solo pair.
