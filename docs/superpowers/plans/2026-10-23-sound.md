# Sound (Plan 3a) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the placeholder voices with real instruments for every role and set, plus the lo-fi chain and the
bed, built and judged on the host while the FM-1 is unavailable.

**Architecture:** `app/engine/` grows from one placeholder file into:
- a patch table: one patch per `INS_*`, built on six cheap voice models
- a drum kit per `KIT_*`
- the lo-fi chain: dust, surface noise, ambience, and a small plate reverb

`vx_configure(roll)` sets a song's instruments, kit and chain at song start. A host cost bench compares our heaviest
mix to AMB-1's engine, which is known to run on the FM-1. Every firmware build checks memory exactly.

**Scope:** This plan builds the **fallback rungs** of the spec's ladders (FM, Karplus–Strong, simple synthesis), which
are cheap and safe without a device to measure on. The **first-choice rungs** (Plaits and Rings ports) become
Plan 3b, after the device bench settles each role's budget (spec, Building it → Order, step 2).

**Tech Stack:** C99, float only (single-precision FPU), no libm (fm1-base fastmath), no malloc. Host tests via
`PATH=$PWD/.venv/bin:$PATH sh tests/run_tests.sh`; firmware memory via `./build.sh`; listening via `./new-song.sh`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-vnl-1-design.md` (Sound; Building it; Constraints; Testing).

## Global Constraints

- **Hardware:** FM-1, JieLi AC79 single core at 360 MHz, 44.1 kHz stereo; 581 KB flash app slot, 96 KB RAM
  (.data+.bss), 336 KB buffer pool (`EN_POOL`-style placement for big buffers).
- **Audio thread:** sound renders in the audio interrupt. Planning and configuring happen on the main loop or at song
  start. No allocation and no libm in the render path.
- **Licensing:** VNL-1 stays MPL-2.0. Code may come from AMB-1 (MPL, `ref/fm1-amb`) with attribution in the file
  header. DUB-1, LOF-1/HYP-1, MAKU and other GPL firmwares: ideas only.
- **Memory budget (my call until the device says otherwise):** image ≤ 400 KB (room left for Plan 4's screens), RAM
  .data+.bss ≤ 80 KB, pool ≤ 300 KB. Baseline today: 123,624 B; 31,768 B; 69,384 B.
- **CPU budget (my call):** the heaviest rolled song's host render time ≤ 1.0× AMB-1's heaviest piece on the same
  machine (Task 1's bench). It's a proxy; the device bench replaces it.
- **Levels:** the scenario's peak stays −9…0 dBFS; no level jumps between sections or songs (spec Testing).
- **Determinism:** the same seed renders the same samples (noise from seeded generators, not time).

## Review Focus

1. **Denormals and NaN in long tails.** Reverb and filters decaying toward zero can go denormal (slow on the device)
   or NaN on bad coefficients. Every voice and the chain need a silence/NaN check after long renders (Tasks 2, 7).
2. **Voice stealing clicks.** Rich patches with long releases (pads, bells) run out of voices: a steal must fade, not
   click (the placeholder's 4 ms fade is the bar).
3. **Song-to-song carryover.** `vx_configure` at a song change must not cut a ringing reverb or bed abruptly, nor leave
   the last song's instruments on notes still releasing.
4. **Dark sections.** Intro/outro "dark" was promised as a sound treatment (a filter) in Plan 3; it must reach the
   voices per step without changing notes.
5. **Heaviest combination.** The cost bench must use the worst real roll (densest comping, pad and reverb, rain), not
   an average song.

### Task 3: Keys and pads (M_EP, M_PAD)

### Task 7: The lo-fi chain and the bed

**Files:** Create `app/engine/fx.c`, `app/engine/fx.h`; Modify `voices.c` (the mix); Test: `tests/host/song_test.c`

**Interfaces:**
- `void fx_configure(int dust, int noise, int ambience, int flavor);`
- `void fx_process(float *l, float *r, uint32_t n, float duck);`. `duck` is the kick's envelope, for light ducking.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests:**
  - **Dust 0–3:** energy above 6 kHz in white noise through the chain falls as dust rises (0 > 1 > 2 > 3).
    Wow/flutter at dust ≥ 1 detunes a 440 Hz sine by 2–15 cents peak, at 0.3–6 Hz.
  - **Surface noise:**
    - Vinyl gives clicks at 0.5–8 a second plus a rotation tick every 1.8 s ±5%.
    - Tape gives hiss with no clicks.
    - None gives no added energy: the bed is silent alone.
  - **Ambience rain:** noise energy when on, none when off. Gusts are slow (envelope period ≥ 3 s).
  - **Plate reverb:**
    - An impulse decays below −60 dB within 4 s.
    - No denormals: after 10 s of silence, every buffer is exactly 0 or ≥ 1e-20.
    - No NaN after 60 s of random input.
  - **Levels:** the bed sits −30…−18 dB under the music's RMS.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them to see them fail.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement**
  - **Dust:** a low-pass at 5 / 3.5 / 2.5 / 1.5 kHz; wow/flutter through a short modulated delay; tanh-like soft
    saturation (fastmath); bit and sample-rate grit at dust ≥ 2; kick ducking.
  - **Surface noise, ambience and plate:**
    - Vinyl and tape surfaces, and rain (low-passed noise wash, droplet ticks, slow gusts), all from seeded noise.
    - AMB-1's Dattorro plate (MPL, attributed), sized down to fit the pool, at a small send.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run all tests; all pass.** Record the cost bench and memory.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit:** `feat(engine): the lo-fi chain, surface noise, rain and plate`

### Task 7b: Per-instrument chorus and delay, and section filter moves (added mid-run, Sam)

Sam: "Will we be applying fx to instruments as well? If we didn't plan on that, we should." and "reverb, chorus, and
delay seem nice to have". Reverb is per role from Task 7 (a send level per role). This task adds chorus and delay.

**Files:** Modify `app/engine/fx.c/.h`, `app/engine/patch.h/.c` (per-patch amounts), `app/engine/voices.c` (role
buses); Test: `tests/host/song_test.c`

**Interfaces:**
- `vx_patch_t` gains `float chorus, delay;`, each 0–1: chorus depth on the role's bus, and the send to the delay.
- One shared stereo chorus on the chords role's bus, with depth from the chords patch: Rhodes, Wurli and pad high;
  felt piano and nylon low.
- One shared tape delay as a send. Lead and candy patches get nonzero sends; keys get a little. The delay time follows
  the song's tempo (a dotted 8th, from `RF_TEMPO` in `vx_configure`), with feedback ~0.35, a low-pass in the loop and
  a little wow. The buffer is in the pool, at a reduced rate if memory requires it (AMB-1's tape runs at 11 kHz).

- **Section filter moves** (Sam: "yes" to filter as a musical move; the live filter knob is Plan 4):
  - `void vx_set_filter(int role, float amount);` with amount 0 open … 1 closed, one ramped low-pass per role bus
    (chords, bass, lead, candy, drums). It replaces `vx_set_dark` (the dark treatment becomes a filter setting).
    `step()` in `app.c` sets every role each step from the plan block. The mapping is my call, recorded here:
    - **Intro and outro** (`PB_DARK`): drums and bass 0.7, keys, lead and candy 0.4.
    - **Breaks** (`SEC_BREAK`): every role 0.8, sweeping open to 0 across the break's last bar, into the drop.
    - **A drum-drop bar** (`PB_DROP_LAST`): the whole band 0.6 for that bar ("through the wall").
    - **A swell** (`PB_SWELL`, "sound in Plan 3"): every role sweeps from 0.5 to 0 over the bar before the next block.
    - Elsewhere 0. Ramps take about 50 ms, except the sweeps, which follow the bar.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests:**
  - **Section filters:**
    - Break bars carry less energy above 2 kHz than groove bars of the same song.
    - Across a break's last bar, that energy rises monotonically, measured per beat.
    - In intro bars, the drums lose more of it than the keys.
    - Notes are unchanged: the event stream is identical with and without the filters.
  - **Chorus:** a held Rhodes chord with chorus > 0 has left/right correlation < 0.95 and a slow pitch wobble
    (0.2–2 Hz, 2–15 cents). Chorus 0 leaves L = R for a centered source.
  - **Delay:**
    - A lead impulse echoes at the dotted-8th time ±1 ms for the song's tempo, with each repeat quieter than the last.
    - Repeats fall below −60 dB within 3 s.
    - A send of 0 gives no echo.
  - **Safety:** no NaN or denormals after 60 s of random input through both.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them to see them fail.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.**
  - **Chorus:** two modulated taps on a short delay line (≤ 30 ms), linear interpolation, LFO at control rate.
  - **Delay:** a feedback loop with a one-pole low-pass, and slow wow on the read head.
  - **Song changes:** the delay's tail survives them, like the reverb's.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run all tests; all pass.** Record the cost bench (picker plus seeds 20, 100, 183) and memory. Both stay
  in budget. If they don't, ledger what to lighten and lighten it, starting with the delay's sample rate.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit:** `feat(engine): chorus on the keys, tape delay on leads and candy, section filter moves`
