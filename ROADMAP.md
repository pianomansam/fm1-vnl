# Roadmap

Ideas for VNL-1 after its first release. Nothing here is promised: each idea gets built, heard on the FM-1, and kept
only if it earns its place. Near-term items are in progress or next in line; later ones are planned; maybe ones are
ideas that still need a reason.

## Near-term

- **A fade between the two home screens:** HOME cross-fades the now-playing and main screens, as the splash fades in.
- **A stutter on demand:** on the Sections screen, a control queues a stutter for the next group's last bar.
- **A stutter where no lead plays:** the comp catches the stutter in sections without a lead.
- **Broken-chord piano comp:** a climbing broken chord for the acoustic piano, in place of a strum.
- **Soft synth lead:** a candidate lead for the synth sets, waiting on a listen.
- **Settings screen:** reduced motion, the screen reader, key lights and brightness.
- **Device checks:** confirm the CPU guard's voice rungs and the per-stage CPU meter on the FM-1 itself.

## Later

- **A B section:** a second progression in the song, where the comp, lead, register or drums may change.
- **Drummer layer:** accented eighth hats, two-bar variation, tom fills and a crash for the acoustic kits.
- **More grooves:** a second bossa-style pattern, a straight city-pop groove and a drum machine for chillwave.
- **A ride for the synth kits:** a clap layer, or the hats carrying the ride's role.
- **Song energy over time:** a curve that shapes how busy and loud each section is.
- **Free-flowing mode:** play sections by hand with no song map, record the take and play it back.
- **Sound pages:** ENV opens a part's sound (filter, envelope, effects) on the device.
- **Organ controls:** drawbars, percussion, key click and rotary speed; then the organ as a lead.
- **Lead refinements:** color tones on strong beats, contour shapes, a cap on busy passages, two leads in A-B-A.
- **Comping refinements:** a slow top line in the keys, chord changes that land a little late, cells varied per pass.
- **Browser device UI:** the FM-1's screens in the browser over USB, replacing the workbench in the long run.
- **Advanced rules:** a settings page that switches the engine's rules on and off.
- **Patterns on the device:** drum and comp patterns and song layouts made on the FM-1, shared through a website.

## Maybe

- **Synth cello:** parked; it could come back with body resonances.
- **Sampled sax and trumpet leads:** parked (2026-11-13). Three rounds from CC0 libraries (a tenor and an alto sax;
  Harmon, straight-mute and open trumpets; soft and loud takes) all sounded too mellow, not reedy or brassy enough,
  in the few kilobytes a lead gets in the sample bank. They could come back with more sample space.
- **More sampled instruments:** marimba, kalimba, harmonica, hand percussion, from CC0 libraries.
- **More leads:** a string ensemble, the Rhodes or nylon guitar as leads, or a sampled violin.
- **Texture:** birdsong in the bed, tape warble per instrument.
- **Effects:** a spring reverb, a rotary speaker for the organ comp.
- **Drum details:** phrase-end fills, a three-against-four shaker, hats that skip now and then, swing tied to tempo.
- **Bass details:** rhythm cells with anticipations and ghost notes, a contour-led walking bass.
- **Piano-roll editing:** edit a part's notes in the workbench, with a note on why.
- **Learning from likes:** songs you like steer future rolls; "another like this", with undo.
- **The 909 and SDS-V:** FOMAJAM's other modeled machines, already in the code and unused.
